Diario Support

For help, feedback, or privacy requests, email info@boliapps.com.

Start practicing

Sign in, choose the language used for explanations, and add the languages and levels you want to practice. Write a short story, have a typed roleplay, or choose a voice conversation.

Practice again

After finishing an exercise, use the practice-again button on the home screen or lesson review to start another exercise.

AI permission

Diario asks before sharing exercise content and voice audio with OpenAI. You can decline, read saved lessons, or manage your account. Review or withdraw permission in Settings. AI feedback is a learning aid and can make mistakes.

Microphone or connection problems

Voice practice needs internet access and microphone permission. Check Settings → Apps → Diario → Microphone on your iPhone. If a conversation disconnects, return to the lesson and reconnect. Internet access is also needed for writing feedback and typed roleplays.

Delete your account

Open Diario → Settings → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account and active exercise data. If sign-in account deletion is temporarily unavailable, it retries automatically. See the Privacy Policy for retention details. You can also contact us for help. Signing out alone does not delete your account.

App Store subscriptions

Manage any Diario subscription in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting your Diario account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, iOS version, Diario version, and a short description. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or sensitive practice content.
