Diario Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Diario is a language-practice app developed by Boli Apps. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use the iOS app and website. Contact info@boliapps.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we process

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, and authentication information managed by Clerk. Your sign-in provider may also provide a name or profile image to Clerk.
  • Practice content: your writing, roleplay messages, custom scenario descriptions, voice transcripts, generated lessons, corrections, scores, and progress assessments.
  • Preferences and activity: your languages and levels, exercise dates and completion, streaks, voice usage, and AI permission choice.
  • Voice audio: when you choose voice practice and allow microphone access, audio is transmitted directly from your device to OpenAI. Diario stores transcripts, not raw voice recordings.
  • Technical and support information: request identifiers, requested API paths, errors, response times, client type, and information you choose to send to support. Hosting and authentication providers may process network and device information needed to operate and secure their services.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, provide and save exercises, generate AI replies and feedback, show your learning progress, enforce usage limits, answer support requests, and operate and secure the service. We do not sell your personal information, display third-party advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

AI processing and your choice

Before AI practice, Diario asks for permission to share data with OpenAI. This includes your exercise text, chat messages, voice transcripts, custom scenarios, relevant lesson history, and language and level settings. Voice practice also sends microphone audio for transcription and spoken replies. We do not intentionally include your account email or sign-in credentials in AI prompts.

You may decline and continue to read saved lessons or manage your account. Withdraw permission in Settings → Withdraw AI permission. Withdrawal stops new AI requests and pending progress analysis; it cannot recall requests already sent. Permission does not replace the separate iOS microphone permission.

OpenAI’s API data is not used to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in. OpenAI may retain content for abuse monitoring, normally for up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its API data controls. Provider retention is separate from Diario’s storage. AI replies and feedback may be inaccurate.

Service providers and international processing

  • Clerk provides account authentication and session management, including the sign-in providers you select.
  • OpenAI provides exercise generation, feedback, learning analysis, transcription, and voice conversations.
  • Fly.io hosts the Diario API and database in the United States.
  • Vercel hosts the web app and these public information pages. GitHub hosts the source repository and a GitHub Pages copy of these pages.

These providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate. Their privacy and security practices apply to their services.

Storage and deletion

We keep your account, exercises, transcripts, feedback, and progress while your account remains active. Go to Settings → Delete account to request permanent deletion. Diario removes your active application data and requests deletion of your Clerk sign-in account. If Clerk is temporarily unavailable, the request is queued and retried automatically.

We retain a minimal deletion record containing your account identifier and deletion timestamps to prevent old authentication tokens from recreating the account and to complete retries. Restricted backup copies and operational records may remain until they expire or are removed under the hosting providers’ retention policies. They are not available through the app. Contact us about access, correction, or deletion requests, including backup or provider-held data.

Signing out clears this device’s loaded account data, API cache, and shared widget snapshot. Signing out does not delete your server account. If you have an App Store subscription, manage or cancel it through Apple; deleting an app account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Children

Diario is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it. Do not put sensitive personal information about yourself or others in practice exercises.

Changes and contact

We will publish updates here with a revised effective date. Material changes to AI sharing will require a new permission choice. For privacy requests or questions, email info@boliapps.com.
