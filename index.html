Find your voice.

Diario is a thoughtful daily practice for expressing yourself in another language, in writing and aloud.

Write about your world

Start with a short prompt and build a story in the language you are learning. Review corrections in context.

Practice real conversations

Explore everyday situations through typed and spoken roleplays, with scenarios shaped to your language and level.

Notice your progress

Revisit saved lessons, understand recurring patterns, and build a quiet practice rhythm.

AI practice requires internet access and your permission to share content with OpenAI. Voice practice also requires microphone permission.
